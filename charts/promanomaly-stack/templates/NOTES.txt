promanomaly-stack installed.

The umbrella deploys:
  - promanomaly  (detector)
  - victoria-metrics-single (TSDB)

Once both pods are Ready, port-forward the detector to inspect /metrics:
  kubectl --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/{{ .Release.Name }}-promanomaly 9092:9092

Configure detector groups under ``promanomaly.groups`` in your values.
See the promanomaly chart README for the full value reference.
